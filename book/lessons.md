# Free Biomechanics Lessons

These open lessons are designed to help educators and students investigate biomechanics through accessible explanations, demonstrations, and hands-on activities. Materials are free to read, adapt, and share under the [CC BY 4.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), unless noted otherwise.

## Browse by topic

### Cardiovascular system

Explore the mechanics of blood flow, the cardiovascular system, medical imaging, sensors, and computational approaches.

**[Open cardiovascular lessons](Cardiovascular/overview.md)**

### Human locomotion

Learn about movement and locomotion, including the principles behind passive dynamic walking.

**[Open human locomotion lessons](Human%20locomotion/overview.md)**

### Robotics

Investigate connections between biomechanics, movement, and robotic systems.

**[Open robotics lessons](Robotics/overview.md)**

## What each lesson includes

Lesson pages identify the intended audience, approximate time, group size, materials, and technology needs. Where applicable, they also include printable resources, code, software, and files for making physical teaching tools.

If you have questions about using or adapting a lesson, contact [biomechday@andrew.cmu.edu](mailto:biomechday@andrew.cmu.edu).
